About this public snapshotWhat is included, and how it is reviewed

Only information retrieved from anonymously accessible public sources is published. Microsoft roadmap features use Microsoft's public roadmap API. Blog entries are grouped by public article, not by private annotations.

The Ignite 2026 filter includes individual announcements and 242 public sessions verified against Microsoft's public Book of News, product blogs, public roadmap, Copilot Credits Guide (PDF), session catalog, and labelled Message Center mirrors. Session cards include a concise summary, expandable takeaways, and an official video link where verified. Briefs use public descriptions and, where labelled, Microsoft's AI-generated summaries; they are not transcript-complete reviews or release commitments. Several announcements may share a source.

Message Center links point to MS Message Center, an independent third-party mirror, not the authenticated Microsoft 365 admin center. Mirror content may lag or differ from the original announcement.

No NDA material, internal notes, private customer details, assignees, personal contact information, private links, credentials, or tenant-specific messages are included. Unverified or unavailable sources are omitted. Roadmap estimates are not commitments; consult the linked source for current details.
